Both font files were taken from the @fontsource packages and are served from
this folder, so the site loads them from its own domain. No external font
service is contacted.

Files in this folder:
  inter-latin-wght-normal.woff2
  inter-latin-ext-wght-normal.woff2
  space-grotesk-latin-wght-normal.woff2
  space-grotesk-latin-ext-wght-normal.woff2

The latin-ext files are included because they contain the rupee sign (U+20B9)
used in the entry fee text.
