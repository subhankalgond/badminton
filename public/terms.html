Terms and Conditions

These terms apply to every team that registers for the inter-college men's doubles badminton tournament. Submitting the registration form means both players accept them.

1. Eligibility

  • The tournament is for men's doubles. Each team has exactly two players.
  • Both players in a team must belong to the same college. Teams with players from different colleges are not allowed and will not be accepted.
  • The college name entered by both players must match. Capitalisation and extra spaces are ignored during the check.
  • Both players must be able to prove their college identity when the organizer asks for it.

2. Entry fee

  • The entry fee is ₹300 per team. It covers both players.
  • The fee is paid by UPI using the QR code shown on the registration page.
  • The amount is fixed by the organizer. The server records ₹300 for every registration.

3. Registration and payment verification

  • Every registration is stored with the status PENDING.
  • The pending team must upload a screenshot of the payment as proof. Accepted formats are JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP, with a maximum size of 5 MB.
  • Uploading a screenshot does not confirm a registration. The organizer verifies each payment by hand.
  • A registration becomes ACCEPTED only after the organizer verifies the ₹300 payment and accepts the team. The verification date, time and the organizer account are recorded.
  • The organizer may reject a registration with a recorded reason, for example a fake or invalid screenshot, payment not received, an incorrect amount, an unclear screenshot, a different college, or a duplicate registration.

4. Duplicate and multiple entries

  • One team name can be used only once.
  • One email address can appear in only one team.
  • The same pair of players cannot be registered twice.

5. Data provided by the team

  • Team name, player names, email addresses, college names and the payment screenshot are collected for this tournament only.
  • The payment screenshot is stored on the organizer's server and is visible only to the signed in organizer.
  • See the privacy notice for details.

6. Conduct

  • False information, forged screenshots or impersonation lead to rejection of the team.
  • The organizer decides the match schedule, rules of play and the final list of accepted teams.
  • Any clarification about a registration must be raised with the organizer before the tournament begins.
