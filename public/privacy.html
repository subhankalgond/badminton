Privacy notice

This page explains exactly what the registration form collects, where it is stored and who is allowed to see it.

What is collected

  • Team name.
  • Full name, email address, mobile number and college name of both players.
  • The payment screenshot you upload as proof of the ₹300 entry fee.
  • The date and time of your submission, and the verification or rejection details recorded by the organizer.

Why it is collected

  • To register the team for the tournament.
  • To contact the players about the schedule and other tournament information. The mobile number is used to reach a player quickly on the day of the event.
  • To confirm that both players belong to the same college.
  • To check that the ₹300 entry fee has been paid.

Who can see it

  • The organizer signs in with a username and password to view registrations.
  • Registration data and payment screenshots are never shown to the public.
  • Payment screenshots are served only to a signed in organizer account, and never as public files.
  • No data is shared with any third party, advertising network or analytics service.

How it is stored

  • Registration details are stored in the tournament database on the organizer's server.
  • Payment screenshots are stored as files with random names in a private folder. They are not reachable by URL without signing in.
  • Only image files in JPG, JPEG, PNG or WEBP format up to 5 MB are accepted. The file signature is checked, so other file types are refused.

Cookies

  • The registration page does not use cookies.
  • One cookie is set only when the organizer signs in. It keeps the admin session open and is not used for tracking or advertising.

Removal and contact

  • A team that has been rejected does not block the same players from registering again with corrected details.
  • To ask for a correction or the removal of your data after the tournament, contact the organizer through the tournament notice board or the contact details shared with your college.
